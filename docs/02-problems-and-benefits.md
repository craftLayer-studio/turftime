# 02 Problems and benefits: TurfTime

Format: problem, then TurfTime solution, then benefit and the metric it moves.

## Owner

| Problem | Solution | Benefit | Metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| Double bookings from phone, WhatsApp and marketplace in parallel | One calendar with slot holds; staff app blocks marketplace bookings in two taps | No clashes, no angry teams, no refunds | Retention (owner), revenue |
| No-shows on phone bookings | Online advance payment with cancellation policy | Revenue protected; slots released early can be resold | Revenue |
| Marketplace commission, widely estimated at 10–15% per booking ([Idea Usher](https://ideausher.com/blog/develop-sports-ground-booking-app-like-playo/), accessed 2026-11-24) | Own booking link at 0% commission; move repeat players to it | Keep the margin on repeat bookings | Revenue |
| Marketplace owns player data | Venue CRM with consent; every split-pay player captured | Direct relationship; offers without paying for discovery | Retention |
| Empty weekday afternoons | Automated fill offers to past players; off-peak price rules | Higher occupancy on dead hours | Revenue |
| Phone rings late at night for bookings | 24x7 self-serve booking | Time back for the owner | Activation |
| No view of which hours and sports earn | Occupancy heatmap and revenue reports | Better pricing and staffing decisions | Revenue |
| High capex to recover: building a turf can cost ₹15–50 lakh ([ThePrint](https://theprint.in/ground-reports/gurugram-box-cricket-business-farmland/3080271/), accessed 2026-11-24) | Peak pricing, packs and tournaments raise revenue per hour | Faster payback on the turf investment | Revenue |

| Problem | Solution | Benefit | Metric |

| Not knowing about off-peak deals or tournaments | Opt-in WhatsApp offers | More play for less | Revenue |

## Money summary for the owner (example, estimate)

A 2-pitch box cricket turf at ₹1,200 per hour, open 16 hours, at 50% occupancy earns about ₹19,200 per day, or about ₹5.8 lakh per month (estimate). If 200 bookings a month come through a marketplace at 10–15% commission, the owner pays ₹24,000–₹36,000 per month in commission (estimate from the third-party commission range). Moving even half of those repeat players to TurfTime saves ₹12,000–₹18,000 per month. Add 10 points of off-peak occupancy at a discounted ₹800 per hour, about 3 extra hours a day across 2 pitches: about ₹72,000 per month (estimate). TurfTime Growth costs ₹2,999. Sales line: **"TurfTime pays for itself with three bookings a month."**
