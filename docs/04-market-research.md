### Differentiation

| | Marketplace (Playo, KheloMore) | Free venue tool (Hudle) | Global SaaS (CourtReserve) | **TurfTime** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Brings new players | Yes | Yes | No | No (complements marketplaces) |
| Venue owns player data | No | Partly (estimate) | Yes | Yes |
| Commission | Yes (estimate 10–15%) | 0% claimed | 0% | 0% |
| Split UPI payment | Limited (estimate) | Unknown | No | Yes (P1) |
| WhatsApp-native reminders and fill offers | Limited (estimate) | Unknown | SMS/email | Yes |
| Price | Commission | Unclear | ₹17,500+ per month | ₹1,499–₹5,499 per month |
| On-ground setup support | Varies | Varies | No | Yes, done-for-you |

**Positioning:** "Keep Playo for new players. Use TurfTime for everyone who comes back." The repeat-player channel is where commission hurts most and where owner-owned CRM pays off.

## Owner pain points

1. Double bookings across phone, WhatsApp and marketplace (SlotTurf leads with double-booking prevention, which signals the pain; [SlotTurf](https://slotturf.pages.dev/)).
2. Commission on repeat players who would have booked directly anyway ([Idea Usher](https://ideausher.com/blog/develop-sports-ground-booking-app-like-playo/); rates unverified).
3. High capex (₹15–50 lakh) puts pressure on occupancy and pricing ([ThePrint](https://theprint.in/ground-reports/gurugram-box-cricket-business-farmland/3080271/)).
4. Late-night demand and staffing (ThePrint describes turfs earning from late-night bookings).
5. No-shows and collecting money from groups (estimate; validate in owner interviews).

Caveat: no public dataset on no-show rates or commission rates. Collect both in the first 30 owner interviews and update this doc.
