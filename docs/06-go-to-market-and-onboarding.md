# 06 Go-to-market and onboarding: TurfTime

## Positioning statement

For independent turf, court and gaming cafe owners in India who lose money to double bookings, no-shows and marketplace commissions, TurfTime is a zero-commission booking link and QR with UPI advance payment, split pay and WhatsApp reminders. Unlike marketplaces, the venue owns its players; unlike global court software, it costs under ₹3,000 a month and is set up for you in a day.

## Sales script (5 minutes, at the venue)

1. **Opener:** "Bhaiya, how many bookings came through Playo/Hudle last month, and how many of those teams had played here before?" (Repeat players on a marketplace is the pain.)
2. **Pain:** "Every repeat team that books through an app costs you a commission. And phone bookings without advance means no-shows."
3. **Show:** Their pre-built page on the phone: slot grid, peak price, UPI advance, WhatsApp confirmation, split pay.
4. **Math:** "If 20 repeat bookings a month come to your own link, TurfTime pays for itself. Off-peak offers are extra profit."
5. **Close:** "Keep Playo for new teams. Put this QR up for everyone who comes back. 14 days free, we set it up tonight."

## Objection handling

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "Playo/Hudle brings me customers." | "Keep them for discovery. TurfTime is for the teams already standing here. Every one of them gets your link and books direct next time." |
| "Hudle software is free." | "Free tools still keep the players on their app. TurfTime gives you the player list, WhatsApp offers to fill empty slots, and split pay. Try both for 14 days and compare the money." |
| "Players won't pay advance." | "Start with ₹200 advance. Regulars can be marked 'pay at venue'. Advance-paid teams almost never no-show." (Validate with pilots.) |
| "My manager isn't tech-savvy." | "The staff app is one screen: tap a slot, type a phone number. We train him in 10 minutes." |
| "I don't want a monthly fee." | "Then take pay-per-booking: ₹19 per online booking, nothing else. It is still cheaper than any commission." |
| "What if there is a double booking?" | "The calendar locks a slot the moment someone starts paying. If we ever cause a double booking in month one, we refund that slot's value." |

## Activation milestones

- Day 1: QR installed, link shared, first online booking.
- Day 7: ≥30% of bookings via TurfTime (online + staff app); zero double bookings.
- Day 30: ≥70% of bookings via TurfTime; ≥100 players in CRM with consent.

## Retention playbook

| Signal | Action |
|---|---|
| Staff stop entering phone bookings (calendar gaps vs footfall) | Call owner; retrain; WhatsApp nightly "tomorrow's bookings" summary to owner and staff |
| Online share falls below 30% | Re-share link to captains; add QR at dugout; run a "book online, get ₹50 off" week |
| Monsoon slowdown | Push indoor-sport features, packs, and tournament pack offers |
| Owner annual renewal due | "TurfTime year in review": bookings, advance collected, commission avoided (estimate), fill-offer revenue |

## Upsell paths

1. Pay-per-booking to Growth when monthly fees approach ₹2,999 (automatic prompt).
2. Starter to Growth when the owner wants split pay or fill offers (trigger: 100+ players in CRM).
3. Tournament packs during IPL and festival seasons.
4. Pro when adding a second venue, a coaching academy or gaming stations.
5. Floodlight relay integration for electricity savings.
6. Cross-sell CraftLayer: ClassNest for turf-based cricket/football academies; DineLoop for turf cafes.
